Features

Everything a crosshair needs

Built for players who want full control over the reticle without touching game files.

🖼️

PNG / SVG Crosshair Packs

Load ready-made crosshair packs or your own PNG and SVG files. Vector assets stay razor-sharp at every resolution and scale.

⌨️

Hotkey Toggle

Show or hide the overlay instantly with a global hotkey. Bind any key combo and switch crosshairs mid-match without alt-tabbing.

🎯

Per-Game Profiles

Each game gets its own crosshair, position, size and color. Profiles auto-apply when the matching process launches.

📐

DPI-Aware Scaling

Crosshairs stay the same on-screen size across monitors and DPI settings, so your aim feels consistent on every setup.

🎚️

Transparency Tuning

Dial opacity from fully solid to barely-there. Blend the crosshair into bright scenes without losing the center point.
